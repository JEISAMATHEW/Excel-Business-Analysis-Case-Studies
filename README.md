# Excel Business Analysis – Practical Case Studies

Gantt Charts • Sales Forecasting • What-If Analysis • Scenario Comparison

## 📌 Overview

This project contains four practical Excel case studies that simulate real tasks performed by a Business Analyst. The goal isn't just to calculate numbers — it's to use Excel to **understand projects, predict future sales, test different possibilities, and support business decisions**.

**Tool used:** Microsoft Excel

---

## 📊 Case Study 1 – Gantt Chart

A Gantt Chart is a visual project timeline showing what tasks need to be completed, when each task starts and ends, how long it takes, and which activities overlap.

**What I did:**
- Added a Duration column (`End Date − Start Date + 1`)
- Built a Gantt Chart using a Stacked Bar Chart (Start Date as the first series, Duration as the second)
- Formatted the Start Date series as "No Fill" so only task bars are visible
- Reversed the task order on the vertical axis
- Identified the longest task, overlapping tasks, and total project duration

---

## 📈 Case Study 2 – Sales Forecasting

Sales forecasting estimates future sales using historical data to help plan inventory, budgets, and marketing activities.

**What I did:**
- Created a Line Chart of monthly sales
- Identified the sales trend (increasing/decreasing)
- Forecasted the next six months using Excel's Forecast Sheet and `FORECAST.LINEAR()`
- Compared historical vs. forecasted sales on one chart
- Identified the month with the highest actual sales
- Summarized what the forecast means for management

---

## 🔀 Case Study 3 – What-If Analysis & Scenario Manager

What-If Analysis answers: *"What happens if I change something?"* — e.g., how profit changes if sales or expenses shift.

**What I did:**
- Calculated Profit using `= Sales − Expenses`
- Built Best Case, Normal Case, and Worst Case scenarios using Scenario Manager
- Used Sales and Expenses as the changing cells
- Generated a Scenario Summary
- Identified the scenario with the highest profit and the greatest business risk
- Created a column chart comparing profit across all three scenarios

---

## 🍔 Case Study 4 – Comparing Business Scenarios (Food Delivery Case)

As a Business Analyst for a food delivery company, I evaluated three possible business situations for the next quarter.

**What I did:**
- Calculated Variable Cost, Total Expenses, Profit, and Profit Margin % for each scenario
- Built charts comparing profit, and comparing Sales vs. Total Expenses
- Identified the scenarios with the highest sales, profit, expenses, and profit margin
- Explained the impact of falling sales and rising variable costs on profit
- Recommended one scenario to management with supporting reasoning

---

## 🧩 The Big Picture

These four skills reflect a real Business Analyst workflow:

| Step | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Gantt Chart | Plan and track the project |
| Sales Forecasting | Predict what may happen in the future |
| What-If Analysis | Ask what happens when inputs change |
| Scenario Manager | Save and compare possible situations |
| Scenario Comparison | Use results to recommend a decision |

**Key takeaway:** A Business Analyst uses Excel not just to calculate numbers, but to understand what is happening, predict what may happen, and help the business decide what to do.
